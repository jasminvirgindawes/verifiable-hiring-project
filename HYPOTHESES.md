# Hypotheses – Verified job applications (jobs.ch × swiyu)

Self-initiated concept, not affiliated with JobCloud. Status is updated after the survey (closes 29 Nov 2026), the Sprint 2 interviews and the Sprint 4 usability tests.

**Status:** ⏳ pending · ✅ supported · ❌ not supported · ➖ partly supported

## Problem hypotheses (survey + interviews, Sprints 1–2)

| # | Hypothesis | Who | Supported when | Status | Stories |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Gathering and re-sending proof (diplomas, Zeugnisse) is slow and frustrating | Applicants | Most rate effort 4–5 or report at least one problem | ⏳ | 10 |
| H2 | Applicants are uneasy that many companies keep copies of their documents | Applicants | Most rate concern 4–5 or don't know what happens to their documents | ⏳ | 2, 9 |
| H3 | Recruiters spend real time checking proof and don't fully trust PDFs | Recruiters | Typical check ≥ 10 min or trust rated 1–3 | ⏳ | 1, 6 |
| H4 | Recruiters would rather keep a verification result than documents | Recruiters | Most say "a relief" or "fine" | ⏳ | 5 |
| H9 | Applicants tailor CV and cover letter for most applications, often with AI: the story changes, the facts stay the same | Applicants | Most adapt often or every time; a meaningful share use AI | ⏳ | 3, 6 |
| H10 | AI-generated or embellished CVs make applications harder to trust, raising the value of verified facts | Recruiters | AI-generated CVs among the top challenges | ⏳ | 4 |
| H11 | Career gaps and non-linear careers are hard to present credibly (exploratory) | Applicants | Several pick it; interviews explain why | ⏳ | 7 |

## Value and solution hypotheses (concept questions now, usability tests in Sprint 4)

| # | Hypothesis | Who | Supported when | Status | Stories |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H5 | Applicants understand what they share and with whom at consent | Applicants | ≥ 4 of 5 explain it correctly after the usability test | ⏳ | 2, 8, 9 |
| H6 | Applicants would use a wallet instead of uploading PDFs | Applicants | Most rate likelihood 4–5 and prefer the concept to jobs.ch today | ⏳ | 2, 3, 7 |
| H7 | Recruiters trust a "verified by [issuer]" badge enough to skip manual checks (incl. a check done by jobs.ch) | Recruiters | Most rate usefulness 4–5 and accept the badge in the test task | ⏳ | 1, 4, 6, 8 |
| H8 | Applying with verified credentials is faster and easier than today | Applicants | Time on task equal or faster and rated easier | ⏳ | 3 |

## Decision gate after Sprint 2

| Result | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| H1, H3, H9, H10 supported | Go: the problem is real for both sides; design the solution |
| H2, H4 supported | Keep data minimisation as part of the value |
| H12 supported | Business case holds; add-on model stays |
| H1 not supported, H3/H10 supported | Focus on the recruiter side; the applicant flow only needs to be easy |
| H2, H4 not supported | Lead with trust and time, not privacy |
| H12 not supported | Explore other models: free for employers, paid ATS integration, fraud prevention for JobCloud |
| H11 strong | Record as a finding and future idea, not part of this MVP |
